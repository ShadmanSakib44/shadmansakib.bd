I am a Software Engineer at Synesis IT PLC in Dhaka, Bangladesh, where I build backend systems in Java and Spring Boot for enterprise-scale government information platforms. I hold a B.Sc. in Software Engineering from the Islamic University of Technology (IUT), and my day-to-day work — maintaining legacy codebases, refactoring for structural consistency, and securing distributed microservices — sits close to the software evolution problems I care about as a researcher.

My research interests center on automated software verification and model conformance, large language models for requirements engineering, and trustworthy, reliable AI. My undergraduate thesis built an agentic, prompt-driven pipeline that generates structured functional requirements from unstructured app-store reviews, evaluating machine-generated artefacts against human baselines to surface systematic conformance gaps. This work was accepted at NLP4RE’26 @ REFSQ, and a related study on LLM reliability in clinical prediction appears in IEEE Xplore.

Feel free to reach out by email, or browse my CV, publications, and projects.

    Shadman Sakib and others

    Abs arXiv Bib HTML
